### Outreach Sequences

#### Sequence 1: Defense Contractor CTO

**Touch 1 - LinkedIn Connection**
"Hi [Name] - Leading AI initiatives at [Company] in aerospace is fascinating. As the engineer who deployed the first LLM in space, I work with defense contractors applying space-grade AI reliability to mission-critical systems. Would love to connect."

**Touch 2 - Value Email (3 days later)**
Subject: Space-grade AI reliability for [Company] defense systems

[Personalized email using template from positioning materials]

**Touch 3 - LinkedIn Engagement (1 week later)**
Like and thoughtfully comment on their recent posts about AI or aerospace

**Touch 4 - Follow-up Email (2 weeks later)**
Subject: Quick question about AI deployment challenges

Brief email asking about their biggest AI deployment challenges, offering to share lessons learned from space deployment

**Touch 5 - Phone Call (1 week later)**
Brief call referencing email conversation, requesting 15-minute exploratory meeting

#### Sequence 2: Government Agency AI Leader

**Touch 1 - Referral Introduction**
Leverage security clearance and government contacts for warm introductions when possible

**Touch 2 - Research-Driven Email**
Reference specific agency AI initiatives or challenges mentioned in public statements

**Touch 3 - Case Study Sharing**
Share relevant (non-classified) insights from space AI deployment that apply to their context

**Touch 4 - Meeting Request**
Request brief conversation about how space-proven AI approaches might support agency objectives

#### Sequence 3: Space Industry Executive

**Touch 1 - Industry Event Connection**
Connect at conferences or through mutual industry connections

**Touch 2 - Technical Deep-Dive**
Offer to share technical insights from space LLM deployment relevant to their missions

**Touch 3 - Partnership Exploration**
Discuss potential collaboration opportunities for space AI advancement

### Conversion Strategy

#### Qualifying Prospects

**Budget Qualification:**
- Project budgets >$25K for meaningful engagement
- Annual AI spending >$100K indicates serious commitment
- Preference for $100K+ initial engagements

**Authority Confirmation:**
- Decision-making authority for AI initiatives
- Budget approval capability
- Technical evaluation responsibility

**Need Validation:**
- Specific AI challenges or opportunities
- Timeline for implementation
- Criticality of reliability/security requirements

#### First Meeting Structure

**Discovery Meeting (30 minutes):**
1. **Introduction (5 min):** Space LLM achievement and credentials
2. **Discovery (15 min):** Their AI challenges, objectives, current approaches
3. **Value Demonstration (5 min):** Relevant insights from space deployment
4. **Next Steps (5 min):** Define follow-up proposal or technical discussion

**Follow-up Proposal Process:**
1. Detailed needs assessment and technical requirements
2. Custom proposal with specific approach and timeline
3. SOW development with clear deliverables and milestones
4. Contract negotiation and engagement kickoff
